# Front I/O Sub-board (`front_io`) — Architecture & Datasheet Design Notes

## 1. CM5108032 (32 GB eMMC) Compatibility, Mirrored `B.Cu` Footprints, Type-B 1:1 FFC & <= 12.5 MHz Default-Speed SDIO Timing
- **RP1 Bank 0 Secondary SDIO (`<= 12.5 MHz`, `80 ns` Period)**: Because the Raspberry Pi Compute Module 5 32 GB eMMC variant (`CM5108032`) routes the dedicated `SDX` pins internally to onboard eMMC, the front-panel push-push SD card socket `J_SD` (`SD-106M`, `C266603`) connects via `J_SUB_FIO` (`FH12-22S-0.5SH`, `C597981`) to the CM5 RP1 GPIO Bank 0 secondary SDIO controller (`GPIO22 = SD_CLK_GPIO22`, `GPIO23 = SD_CMD_GPIO23`, `GPIO24..27 = SD_DAT0..3_GPIO24..27`), with `GPIO5 = SD_PWR_ON`.
  - To accommodate the `~68–75 pF` distributed passive link capacitance (`190 mm` carrier routing + `360 mm` 0.5 mm-pitch FFC + `U7` `SN74CB3Q3245PWR` `C_IO(ON) ~ 8.5 pF` + `front_io` CPWG routing + `RCLAMP3324P` ESD + `J_SD` + `C_CARD`) and `6.8–7.0 ns` round-trip propagation delay across the `597 mm` passive link, all 6 SDIO lines (`SD_CLK`, `SD_CMD`, and `SD_DAT0..3`) are damped at both ends: `33 Ohm` host-side series source-termination resistors `R_SD_CLK`, `R_SD_CMD`, `R_SD_DAT0..3` (`0402WGF330JTCE`, `C25105`) at `U_CM5` pins 46, 47, 45, 41, 24, 48 damp host-driven write/clock edges, while `22 Ohm` card-side series damping and ESD-isolation resistors `R40`, `R12`, `R14`, `R15`, `R37`, `R38` (`0402WGF220JTCE`, `C25092`) placed between `U7.B1..B6` (`/SD_CLK_B`, `/SD_CMD_B`, `/SD_DAT0..3_B`) and `U2`/`U3`/`J_SD` (`/SD_CLK`, `/SD_CMD`, `/SD_DAT0..3`) damp card-driven read transitions and limit residual ESD clamping current into `U7.B1..B6` (`R_out(card) ~ 22 Ohm + R_series = 22 Ohm + R_on(U7) = 4 Ohm ~ 48 Ohm` matched to the FFC/CPWG characteristic impedance, with `<= 4.1 mV` DC voltage drop across the `47 kOhm || 100 kOhm = 32.0 kOhm` pull-up network). The RP1 SDIO controller clock is constrained to `<= 12.5 MHz` (`80 ns` period via CM5 RP1 `mmc` device-tree overlay `max-frequency = <12500000>`), providing `> 48 ns` positive setup margin.
- **Mirrored `B.Cu` Footprints (`J_SUB_FIO`, `SW_ENC`) & Type-B (Opposite-Side Contact, 1-to-1 Pinout) FFC Cable**:
  - `J_SUB_FIO` (`FH12-22S-0.5SH` at `(75.0, 61.9)`, `rot=0`, `B.Cu`) and `SW_ENC` (`EC11J1525402` at `(14.5, 51.0)`, `rot=0`, `B.Cu`) have their local X pad and graphic coordinates mirrored for `B.Cu` mounting (`J_SUB_FIO` Pin 1 at local `x = +5.25 mm` / board `X = 80.25 mm` and Pin 22 at local `x = -5.25 mm` / board `X = 69.75 mm`; `SW_ENC` Pad A at `X = 17.04 mm`, Pad B at `X = 11.96 mm`, Pad D at `X = 17.04 mm`, Pad E at `X = 11.96 mm`).
  - Because `J_FIO` on the carrier board (`F.Cu`, `rot=0`) and `J_SUB_FIO` on `front_io` (`B.Cu`, `rot=0`) are both bottom-contact `FH12-22S-0.5SH` ZIF connectors with 1-to-1 pin numbering (`Pin 1..22 <-> Pin 1..22`), `front_io_cable` is specified as a **Type-B (opposite-side contact, 1-to-1 pinout)** 22-pin `0.5 mm`-pitch FFC cable.
- **G-S-S-G-S-S-G-S-G Interleaved Ground Returns on `J_SUB_FIO.5-13`**:
  - Pin 1, 2: `3V3_CM5` (fed by carrier `3V3_SYS` 2 A buck `U_3V3`)
  - Pin 3: `GND` (G)
  - Pin 4: `SD_CLK_GPIO22` (S)
  - Pin 5: `GND` (G)
  - Pin 6: `SD_CMD_GPIO23` (S)
  - Pin 7: `SD_DAT0_GPIO24` (S)
  - Pin 8: `SDIO_RET` (G — tied to `GND` via parallel `0 Ohm` jumpers `R28` and `R36` `0402WGF0000TCE`, `C17168`)
  - Pin 9: `SD_DAT1_GPIO25` (S)
  - Pin 10: `SD_DAT2_GPIO26` (S)
  - Pin 11: `GND` (G)
  - Pin 12: `SD_DAT3_GPIO27` (S)
  - Pin 13: `SDIO_RET` (G — tied to `GND` via parallel `0 Ohm` jumpers `R28` and `R36` `0402WGF0000TCE`, `C17168`)
  - Pin 14: `SD_PWR_ON`
  - Pin 15: `GND` (G)
  - Pin 16–18: `ENC_A`, `ENC_B`, `ENC_SW` (driven by Schmitt-trigger buffer `U5` through `33 Ohm` series source-termination resistors `R25`–`R27`)
  - Pin 19: `SD_FAULT_VSS_N` (`U1.4 ~{FAULT}` active-low fault interrupt pulled up via `R21 = 10 kOhm` to `3V3_CM5`)
  - Pin 20: `SD_CD_VSS_GPIO6` (driven by Schmitt-trigger buffer `U8.5` `/SD_CD_BUF` through `R16 = 33 Ohm` series source-termination resistor)
  - Pin 21: `SD_WP_VSS_GPIO13` (driven by Schmitt-trigger buffer `U8.7` `/SD_WP_BUF` through `R17 = 33 Ohm` series source-termination resistor)
  - Pin 22, 23, 24: `GND`

## 2. Constant-Current Auto-Retry High-Side SD Power Switch (`U1`), Direct `/SD_VDD`-Sensed Turn-On-Delayed / Fast-Turn-Off 6-Channel SDIO Bus Isolation (`U7`, `U8.3`, `U9`, `D4`, `D5`, `D6`, `Q2`, `Q3`), and Self-Held Active `/SD_VDD` Output Discharge (`Q1`, `Q4`, `R39`, `D2`, `D3`, `C18`, `R29 || R32 || R13`, `R34`)
- **Constant-Current Auto-Retry Switch `U1` (`AP22653W6-7`, LCSC `C2158037`, SOT-23-6)**:
  - Controlled by `SD_PWR_ON` (`U1.3` `EN`, defaulted OFF during boot/reset via `R8 = 10 kOhm` pull-down to `GND` and `C15 = 100 pF` C0G `0402CG101J500NT` `C1546` HF glitch filter).
  - Current limit programmed via `R22 = 56 kOhm` (`1%`, `0402WGF5602TCE`, LCSC `C25796`) on `U1.5` (`ILIM`) to `I_LIM,min = 365.5 mA >= 350 mA`, `I_LIM,typ = 420.6 mA`, `I_LIM,max = 483.5 mA`, providing `> 165 mA` worst-case margin above `200 mA` Default-Speed SD cards while remaining well within the `2.0 A` `TLV62569DBVR` `3V3_SYS` buck and `1.0 A` dual-pin FFC rating.
  - Open-drain `~{FAULT}` (`U1.4`) is pulled up to `3V3_CM5` via `R21 = 10 kOhm` (`0402WGF1002TCE`, `C25744`) and routed over `J_SUB_FIO.19` (`SD_FAULT_VSS_N`) to a CM5 GPIO interrupt.
- **Direct `/SD_VDD`-Sensed, Turn-On-Delayed (`>= 3.05 ms` min / `4.72 ms` typ), `< 150 ns` Brownout/Short & `< 20 ns` Disable/Fault Turn-Off 6-Channel SDIO Bus Isolation (`U7` `SN74CB3Q3245PWR` `C470961`, `R35 = 56 kOhm`, `C14 = 100 nF`, `D4`/`D5`/`D6` `RB751V40T1G` `C154819`, `U8.3`, `U9` `SN74LVC1G97DBVR` `C128411`, Series N-MOSFET NAND Gate `Q3` + `Q2` `BSS138` `C78284`)**:
  - TI `SN74CB3Q3245PWR` (`U7`, LCSC `C470961`, TSSOP-20, 8-bit charge-pump FET bus switch with `r_on = 4 Ohm` typ across `0–3.3 V`, `I_OZ <= 1 uA`, and `I_off <= 1 uA` partial-power-down isolation) sits between the host SDIO lines (`A1..A6` = `/SD_CLK_GPIO22..SD_DAT3_GPIO27`, each source-terminated at `U_CM5` on the carrier board with `33 Ohm` resistors `R_SD_CLK`, `R_SD_CMD`, `R_SD_DAT0..3`) and the card-side lines (`B1..B6` = `/SD_CLK_B`, `/SD_CMD_B`, `/SD_DAT0..3_B` driving `22 Ohm` card-side series damping and ESD-isolation resistors `R40`, `R12`, `R14`, `R15`, `R37`, `R38` into `/SD_CLK`, `/SD_CMD`, and `/SD_DAT0..3`). Unused channels `A7, A8, B7, B8` (pins 8, 9, 12, 11) and `NC` (pin 1) are tied to `/GND`, and `VCC` (pin 20) is powered from `/3V3_CM5` with local `100 nF` bypass capacitor `C12` (`C131394`).
  - Active-low `~{OE}` (`U7.19`, net `/SD_BUS_OE_N`) is pulled up to `/3V3_CM5` via `R30 = 470 Ohm` (`0402WGF4700TCE`, `C25117`) and driven low by a series N-MOSFET NAND gate comprising `Q3` (`BSS138`, Gate = `/SD_PWR_EN_AND` from 2-input Schmitt-trigger AND gate `U9.4`, Source = `/GND`, Drain = `/SD_DIS_EN` pulled up to `/SD_VDD` via `R33 = 1 kOhm` `C11702`) in series with `Q2` (`BSS138`, Gate = `/SD_OE_GATE` referenced directly to downstream `/SD_VDD` via `R31 = 1 kOhm` `C11702` + `C16 = 100 pF` C0G `0402CG101J500NT` `C1546`, `tau = 127 ns`, and clamped low on `~{FAULT}` via Schottky diode `D6` `RB751V40T1G` `C154819` from `/SD_OE_GATE` Anode to `/SD_FAULT_VSS_N` Cathode, Source = `/SD_DIS_EN`, Drain = `/SD_BUS_OE_N`).
  - On turn-on, `R35 = 56 kOhm` (referenced directly to `/SD_VDD`) + `C14 = 100 nF` (`tau = 5.60 ms`) delays `U8.6` (`3A` `/SD_PWR_DLY` -> `3Y` `/SD_PWR_DLY_BUF` -> `U9.6` `IN2`) by `3.05–4.72 ms` after `/SD_VDD` rises so `U7` closes only after `U1` finishes its `0.7–1.5 ms` soft-start of `/SD_VDD`.
  - On a `/SD_VDD` short-circuit or brownout, `Q2` gate (`/SD_OE_GATE`) discharges into `/SD_VDD` through `R31 = 1 kOhm` (`tau = 127 ns`), opening `U7` in `< 150 ns` without waiting for `U1`'s `2–20 ms` `~{FAULT}` blanking timer (`t_Blank`), while upstream Schottky diode `D4` (`RB751V40T1G`, Anode = `/SD_PWR_DLY`, Cathode = `/SD_VDD`) immediately discharges `C14 = 100 nF` into `/SD_VDD` (`< 300 ns`). Similarly, on `~{FAULT}` assertion (`/SD_FAULT_VSS_N = LOW`), `D6` clamps `/SD_OE_GATE` low in `< 20 ns` and `D5` (`RB751V40T1G`, Anode = `/SD_PWR_DLY`, Cathode = `/SD_FAULT_VSS_N`) discharges `C14`, ensuring `U7` always waits the full `3.05–4.72 ms` `R35`/`C14` soft-start delay after brownout or fault recovery.
- **Self-Held Disable-Only Mutually Exclusive Active Switched `/SD_VDD` Output Discharge (`Q1`, `Q4` `BSS138` `C78284`, `R39 = 10 kOhm`, `D2`/`D3` `RB751V40T1G` `C154819`, `C18 = 100 nF` `C131394`, `R29 || R32 || R13 = 3x 470 Ohm` `C25117`, `R34 = 10 kOhm` `C25744`)**:
  - Dedicated N-MOSFET inverter `Q4` (`BSS138`, Gate = `/SD_PWR_ON`, Source = `/GND`, Drain = `/SD_PWR_OFF`) has its `10 kOhm` pull-up resistor `R39.1` fed from local diode-OR hold-up rail `/SD_DIS_VCC`, which is supplied by dual Schottky diodes `D2` (`RB751V40T1G`, Anode = `/3V3_CM5`, Cathode = `/SD_DIS_VCC`) and `D3` (`RB751V40T1G`, Anode = `/SD_VDD`, Cathode = `/SD_DIS_VCC`) and buffered by hold-up capacitor `C18 = 100 nF` (`C131394`) to `/GND`.
  - Whenever `/SD_PWR_ON = HIGH` (`3.3 V`), `Q4` turns ON in `< 50 ns` and pulls `/SD_PWR_OFF` (`Q1.1`) LOW (`0 V`), holding `Q1` strictly OFF before and throughout `U1`'s `0.7–1.5 ms` soft-start (as well as during the `3.05–4.72 ms` `U7` turn-on delay window), so `Q1` never fights `U1` or locks `U1` into constant-current fault mode.
  - Whenever `/SD_PWR_ON = LOW` (`0 V`, `U1` disabled), `Q4` turns OFF and `R39 = 10 kOhm` pulls `/SD_PWR_OFF` (`Q1.1`) HIGH (`~3.02 V`) from `/SD_DIS_VCC`. Even when `/3V3_CM5` collapses to `0 V` during system reset/shutdown (`tau = 1.85 ms`) while `U1` blocks reverse current, `D3` charges `C18 = 100 nF` (`3700x` `Q1` `C_iss`) from `/SD_VDD` to `~3.0 V` and `D2`/`D3` reverse-bias as `/SD_VDD` decays, holding `Q1` strongly enhanced (`V_GS ~ 3.0 V`, `R_DS(on) ~ 2.5 Ohm`) all the way to `/SD_VDD = 0.0 V` and discharging `C1 + C2 = 10.1 uF` below `0.5 V` in `3.00 ms` and worst-case `C1 + C2 + C_card = 20.1 uF` below `0.5 V` in `5.96 ms << 10 ms`.

## 3. Non-Snapback 3.3 V TVS on `/SD_VDD` (`D1`), Inline Flow-Through 3.3 V Snapback Signal ESD (`U2`, `U3`, `U4`, `U6`), and Schmitt-Trigger Conditioning on `SD_CD`/`SD_WP` (`U8`) & `SW_ENC` (`U5`)
- **Non-Snapback 3.3 V TVS Diode on `J_SD.4 (/SD_VDD)` (`D1` Prisemi `PTVSLC3D3V3B`, LCSC `C246774`, SOD-323)**:
  - Placed at `(120.0, 37.5)` adjacent to `C1`/`C2` and `J_SD.4` between `/SD_VDD` (pin 1) and `/GND` (pin 2) to clamp user contact ESD strikes on the card power supply pin without snapback latch-up (`V_RWM = 3.3 V`, `V_BR >= 3.8 V`, no snapback holding voltage below `3.3 V`).
- **Inline Flow-Through Semtech `RCLAMP3324P.TNT` Signal ESD Arrays (`U2`, `U3`, `U4`, `U6`, LCSC `C1976652`, DFN2510-10)**:
  - Opposite flow-through pin pairs (`1-10`, `2-9`, `4-7`, `5-6`) are connected inline in both the schematic and PCB so every signal enters on the connector-side pad and exits on the opposite pad with zero stub length:
    - `U2` at `(123.0, 33.5)`: `/SD_CLK` (`U2.10 -> U2.1`), `/SD_DAT0` (`U2.7 -> U2.4`), `/SD_DAT1` (`U2.6 -> U2.5`), unused `U2.2/9` tied to `/GND`.
    - `U3` at `(109.5, 33.5)`: `/SD_DAT2` (`U3.10 -> U3.1`), `/SD_DAT3` (`U3.9 -> U3.2`), `/SD_CMD` (`U3.7 -> U3.4`, reassigned from `U2` to `U3` adjacent to `J_SD.2`), unused `U3.5/6` tied to `/GND`.
    - `U6` at `(131.5, 33.5)`: `/SD_CD` (`U6.10 -> U6.1`), `/SD_WP` (`U6.9 -> U6.2`), unused `U6.4/7` and `U6.5/6` tied to `/GND`.
    - `U4` at `(25.0, 51.0, rot=-90)`: `/ENC_A_SW` (`U4.1 -> U4.10`), `/ENC_B_SW` (`U4.2 -> U4.9`), `/ENC_BTN_SW` (`U4.4 -> U4.7`), unused `U4.5/6` tied to `/GND`.
- **Debounced & Source-Terminated Schmitt-Trigger Conditioning on `SD_CD` / `SD_WP` (`U8` `SN74LVC3G17DCTR` `C18213`, `C13 = 100 nF`, `R6`/`R7` = `10 kOhm`, `R23`/`R24` = `10 kOhm`, `C10`/`C11` = `100 nF`, `R16`/`R17` = `33 Ohm`) and `SW_ENC` (`U5` `SN74LVC3G17DCTR`, `R9`–`R11` = `10 kOhm`, `R18`–`R20` = `10 kOhm`, `C4`–`C6` = `100 nF`, `R25`–`R27` = `33 Ohm`)**:
  - `R6 = 10 kOhm` and `R7 = 10 kOhm` pull up `/SD_CD` (`J_SD.10`) and `/SD_WP` (`J_SD.11`) directly at the switch contacts (`V_OL = 0.00 V` when closed to `GND`), followed by `R23 = 10 kOhm` + `C10 = 100 nF` (`/SD_CD_FILT`, `tau_fall = 1.0 ms`, `tau_rise = 2.0 ms`) and `R24 = 10 kOhm` + `C11 = 100 nF` (`/SD_WP_FILT`) into Schmitt-trigger buffer `U8` (`SN74LVC3G17DCTR`, LCSC `C18213`, decoupled by `C13 = 100 nF` `C131394`, Channel 3 buffering `/SD_PWR_DLY` -> `/SD_PWR_DLY_BUF`), driving `33 Ohm` series source-termination resistors `R16` (`/SD_CD_BUF` -> `/SD_CD_VSS_GPIO6`) and `R17` (`/SD_WP_BUF` -> `/SD_WP_VSS_GPIO13`) across the `360 mm` FFC cable.
